## Our mission

Bloom works to reduce food insecurity and food waste by connecting donated food with people who can use it.

## Our values

**Dignity:** Everyone deserves to be welcomed and respected.

**Community:** Food support works best through lasting local relationships.

**Care:** We handle food, information, and personal stories responsibly.

**Transparency:** We communicate clearly about donations, distribution, and impact.
